Stick, throttle, and split-ear listening — on this Mac.

This is a local high-fidelity trainer for the ASTB-E Performance Based Measures battery: yellow-aircraft tracking with the X-52 stick, vertical tracking with the throttle, and dichotic letter/number streams isolated to the left and right cups. It is built from the public PBM task descriptions, not the proprietary scoring key.

01 · Hardware

Prove the rig

Guided bench for X-52 axes/buttons and true left/right headphone isolation. Do this before any scored run.

02 · Tracking

Chase the target

VTT (throttle, vertical) then ATT (stick, two-axis, inverted Y). Speed steps and adaptive redirects match the published schedule.

03 · Dichotic

Left ear / right ear

Simultaneous tokens. Attend one ear. Even → stick trigger. Odd → throttle thumb. Letters and the other ear are traps.

Plug the X-52 in with a USB-A to USB-C adapter and allow the accessory if macOS asks. Put the segregated headset on, then pick it as the audio output in the bench. Keyboard fallback: WASD/arrows stick, Q/E or R/F throttle, Space = even/stick, Left Shift = odd/throttle, keys 1–6 = T1–T6.
